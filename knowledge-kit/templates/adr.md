# ADR-<NNNN> — <decision in one line>

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by ADR-<NNNN>
- **Date:** <YYYY-MM-DD>
- **Owners:** <seat / person>
- **Touches:** <module / contract / table / boundary>

## Context

The forces at play: what the system does today, the constraint that makes this a decision rather than an
obvious step, and the evidence (file:line, measurement, incident).

## Decision

What we will do, in the present tense.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, what must now be kept true, and the test or probe that will notice
if the decision is quietly reversed.

## Alternatives rejected

Each with the reason. An ADR with no rejected alternative is usually a description, not a decision.
